Privacy Policy

FileFit

Summary

FileFit processes your images, PDFs and QR codes on your device. Your files are never uploaded to our servers, and we do not require an account.

Files you choose

The app only reads files you explicitly pick. Results are saved in the FileFit folder on your phone (Pictures/FileFit or Documents/FileFit) only when you tap Save, or when you turn on Auto-save. Temporary files are stored in the app cache and can be deleted from Settings.

Camera

The camera is used only on the QR scanner screen to read QR codes. Images from the camera are analysed on the device and are not stored or sent anywhere.

Local data

History, favorites, scan history and settings are stored only on your device. You can clear them any time from Settings.

Ads

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use an advertising ID and device information to show and measure ads, according to Google’s policies and your consent choices. Premium removes ads.

Purchases

Premium purchases are handled by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details.

Children

FileFit is a general-audience utility app and is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the contact address in Settings → Contact us.
